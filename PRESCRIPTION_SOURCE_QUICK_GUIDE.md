# Prescription Source - Quick Reference Guide

## What's New?

A new checkbox option has been added to the customer form to indicate the source of the customer's prescription.

## Location

The prescription source option appears in the **Lens Prescription** section when adding or editing a customer.

## Options

Choose one of the following:

1. ✅ **Prescription by Vision Care** - The prescription was provided by your Vision Care clinic
2. ❌ **Prescription not by Vision Care** - The prescription came from an external source

## How to Use

### When Adding a New Customer

1. Go to **Contacts** → **Add Customer**
2. Click **More Info** button
3. Scroll to **Lens Prescription** section
4. Select the appropriate prescription source option
5. Fill in the prescription details
6. Click **Save**

### When Editing a Customer

1. Open the customer edit form
2. Click **More Info** button (if collapsed)
3. Scroll to **Lens Prescription** section
4. The previously selected option will be shown (if any)
5. Change if needed
6. Click **Update**

### When Adding Related Customers

1. In customer edit form, click **Add Another Related Customer**
2. Fill in customer details
3. In **Lens Prescription** section, select prescription source
4. Fill in prescription details
5. Click **Save Related Customer**

## Important Notes

- This field is **optional** - you can leave it unselected
- The selection is saved with the customer's prescription data
- You can change the selection at any time by editing the customer
- Related customers can have different prescription sources than the primary customer

## Where the Data is Stored

The prescription source is stored in the customer's record and can be used for:
- Tracking prescription sources
- Generating reports
- Filtering customers
- Quality control and compliance

## Visual Indicators

- **Vision Care prescriptions**: Blue color with checkmark icon
- **External prescriptions**: Gray color with X icon
